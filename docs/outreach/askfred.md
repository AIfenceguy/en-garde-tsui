# Draft: asking AskFRED (rewritten 2026-09-10, club-only framing)

Why AskFRED first: it is a company with a published developer program (askfred.net/developers),
a public-beta REST API and MCP server, OAuth2 "Connect to AskFRED" for registered third-party
apps, and a stated process: free for personal and club use, and for anything commercial "ask
before you build: contact support and tell us what you have in mind". Its sister site 14meters
republishes all 36 USA Fencing national points lists daily. Nobody publishes a price.

**Where to send it.** support@askfred.net, from the family Gmail (rtsui.jlconcepts). Ricky
signs as a fencing parent. No app name is given; if they ask for one for the OAuth2 screen, the
name and the HTTPS redirect address are decided then.

**Sent 2026-09-10** by Ricky to support@askfred.net from the family Gmail. Reply expected in 1 to 2 business days.

**After sending.** Keep the reply in this folder. Their written answer, with its conditions, is
the permission. Build the reader on the club-use footing meanwhile and charge nobody until the
answer for other clubs is in hand.

**Subject:** A season-planning tool for our club on the AskFRED API: confirming club use, and what a wider licence would cost

Hello AskFRED team,

Your developer page asks people to say what they are building, so here it is. I am a fencing
parent in Southern California with two sons in foil, Y12 and Y14/Cadet. Over the past year I
built a web tool for our family: a training journal and a season planner. It works out which
tournaments are worth entering for a fencer's goals, how the national points add up, what each
trip will cost with flights and hotels, and it keeps each fencer's own bouts and opponents.

Right now it is for our club only. The families at our club will use it free of charge, each
family reading its own AskFRED record plus the public tournament listings. As I read your terms,
that is the "your club's season" use the API is free for. I am writing to confirm that, and to
ask two things before I build further.

1. **The club version.** Each family would connect its own AskFRED account so the tool reads
   only that family's profile, linked fencers with their USFA ids, events, bouts and tournaments,
   read-only, with your ETags honoured and the public listings refreshed once a day on our side
   rather than per page view. If you would rather register it as an OAuth2 application than have
   families use their personal tokens, tell me what you need from me.

2. **If other clubs ask for it later.** A few coaches have already asked. If the tool were one
   day offered to other clubs, either for a club fee or a family subscription, what would you
   charge for the data, and on what basis: a flat licence, a per-club or per-family rate, or a
   revenue share? I am not there yet, but I would rather know your terms now than build toward
   something you would not agree to.

Three questions about data I cannot get from the API today, so the tool reads from the right
place. First, national standings: the tool shows each family its own fencer's rank and points
and a few reference marks, never a list. 14meters carries the 36 lists daily. Could standings
come through the API, or may the tool read 14meters for that, so it never has to read USA
Fencing's own pages for them? Second, official results of USA Fencing national events, which
register outside AskFRED: do you hold them, and if not, is there a source you would point me to?
Third, so I describe things correctly if USA Fencing ever asks: is the USA Fencing data you
carry, membership records and the points lists, provided under an agreement with USA Fencing,
and would your permission cover a club tool's use of it?

I am happy to show you the tool, to report beta issues as I find them, and to change anything
about how it reads your data. Thank you for building the developer program. It is the first
door in the sport that opens.
