# Draft: asking USA Fencing for written permission (rewritten 2026-09-10)

Why this letter exists: usafencing.org adopts the USOPC Terms of Use (teamusa.com/terms-of-use).
Section 5 lets anyone use the site's content for personal, informational, non-commercial
purposes, and says any other copying or "entering into a database" needs permission from an
authorized person in writing. A free club tool that reads a few public pages a day and keeps a
copy is not "personal", so the permission is what makes the daily read clean, and it must be in
hand before anyone is ever charged.

**Where to send it.** USA Fencing's Contact Us page routes general inquiries to
information@usafencing.org and to the Help Center's "Submit a request" form. Send the email and
also paste it into the Help Center form the same day, so it lands in a ticket queue with a
number. The media addresses on that page are not for this. If they route you to the USOPC
(legal@teamusa.org is the address in the Terms), forward the same text there.

**Who signs it.** Best: the club owner or head coach, with Ricky as the second name. A member
club asking to run a free planning tool for its own fencers is an easier yes than an individual
or a startup. Fill in the bracketed parts. Send from the family Gmail (rtsui.jlconcepts) or the
club's address, not from any address tied to a company.

**What to attach.** One screenshot of the Season screen showing a fencer's own standing card and
the Elite sentence, so they can see that no list of theirs appears anywhere. Nothing else.

**After sending.** Keep the reply, whatever it says, in this folder. A yes is the permission.
A "please stop" is complied with the same day by switching the reads to per-family browsers.
Silence after four weeks: send once more, then treat the club version as running on the
personal-use footing until there is an answer, and do not charge anyone until there is one.

**Subject:** Request for written permission to read public member-portal pages for a free club planning tool

Dear USA Fencing team,

I am writing on behalf of [Club name], a USA Fencing member club in the Orange Coast division,
and as a fencing parent. My two sons fence foil, Y12 and Y14/Cadet. Over the past year I built a
small web tool for our family called En Garde: a training journal and a season planner that
works out which tournaments are worth entering, how the points add up, and what each trip will
cost. Several families at our club have asked to use it, and we would like to offer it to them
free of charge.

Part of the tool reads the same public pages of member.usafencing.org that any parent reads by
hand: the national points pages (for example /points/national/MF/Y14), the ranking pages, a
tournament's event list with entrant counts, and an event's posted results. Your Terms of Use
allow personal use of that content and ask that anything beyond it be permitted in writing. I am
asking for that permission for the following, and nothing more:

- About ten public pages a day, read once and cached, with an identifying user agent
  (EnGardeInsight/1.0) so you can always see it is us.
- The copy is used only to show each family its own fencer's standing, the points at a few
  ranks, and an entrant count. No ranking list, results list, or other USA Fencing content is
  ever displayed or redistributed, and USA Fencing is credited as the source on screen.
- We will change the user agent, the frequency, or the pages, or stop reading any page you name,
  the day you ask.

Two further questions, so we plan correctly:

1. If the tool is one day offered to families outside our club for a subscription, is there a
   licence or data agreement USA Fencing offers for that, and what would its terms and cost be?
   We would rather pay for a proper feed than read pages.
2. If USA Fencing already has an official feed or API it would prefer we use instead of the
   pages, we will switch to it.

If this request belongs with someone else at USA Fencing or with the USOPC, I would be grateful
for the name. Thank you for the work you do for the sport, and for your time.

Sincerely,

[Club owner or head coach name], [title], [Club name]
[Ricky's name], parent, [Club name], Orange Coast division
